This audit evaluates every page title against search engine pixel width thresholds (500–600px / ~50–60 characters), SERP truncation risks, search intent alignment, and AI citation eligibility.

---

## Executive Summary & SEO Health Index

### The Title Tag Problem
Title tags are the **#1 on-page SEO ranking signal** and the primary driver of search engine click-through rates (CTR). In modern **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** across ChatGPT Search, Perplexity AI, Claude, and Google AI Overviews, concise, entity-dense titles dictate whether an AI search assistant cites your content as an authoritative source.

An audit of **60 total pages** (15 static/service pages and 45 published blog articles) revealed a severe **bimodal distribution**:
1. **Critically Long Blog Titles (75.5% of blog posts):** Most blog articles feature lengthy, two-part academic titles exceeding **85 to 113 characters** (e.g., *113 characters on Trezor breach*). In Google search results, titles over 60 characters get cut off with ellipses (`...`), hiding the most compelling value propositions from human searchers and burying core entities.
2. **Under-Optimized Static Pages (60% of core pages):** Core service and trust pages feature minimalist titles under **35 characters** (e.g., *"Our Work | Launch Live Studio"* [29 chars] or *"UI/UX Design | Launch Live Studio"* [33 chars]), failing to leverage commercial modifiers like *"Custom"*, *"Enterprise"*, or *"Design Systems"*.
