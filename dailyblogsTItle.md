## Daily Blog Writing & SEO Standards Checklist

To guarantee every daily blog post achieves maximum organic rankings, Google Discover visibility, and conversion efficacy, every post must satisfy the following criteria:

### 1. Structure & Formatting Standards
*   **H1 Title:** Clear, authoritative, keyword-rich title with high CTR curiosity or clear business outcome.
*   **TL;DR Blockquote:** 2-3 sentence executive summary immediately under H1 with internal links to relevant LaunchLive Studio services.
*   **The 5 W's Framework:** Dedicated section answering Who, What, Where, When, and Why.
*   **Visual / Architectural Diagrams:** ASCII or Mermaid flowcharts visualizing data pipelines, system designs, or workflows.
*   **Comparison & Benchmark Tables:** Markdown tables comparing tech stacks, architectures, costs, or conversion metrics.
*   **Before & After Scenarios:** Real-world transformation narratives highlighting operational friction vs automated success.
*   **5 Detailed FAQs:** Structured Q&A matching Google People Also Ask (PAA) queries for Schema rich snippet eligibility.
*   **Clear Call-to-Action (CTA):** Closing paragraph directing readers to [`/book-a-call`](https://www.launchlive.studio/book-a-call), [`/services`](https://www.launchlive.studio/services), or [`/work`](https://www.launchlive.studio/work).

### 2. Technical SEO & Schema Integration
*   **Word Count:** 1,800 to 3,000 words of dense, non-fluff, highly actionable engineering and business content.
*   **Slug Format:** Lowercase, hyphen-separated, keyword-focused (e.g., `enterprise-rag-architecture-eliminate-hallucinations-secure-data`).
*   **Metadata Integration:** Proper entry in `lib/blog-data.ts` with `title`, `description`, `category`, `readTime`, `image` (Unsplash high-res 1200x630), and `tags`.
*   **Schema.org Validation:** Ensure automated JSON-LD `BlogPosting` and `BreadcrumbList` schemas render on each post page.
